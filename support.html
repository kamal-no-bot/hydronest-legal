HydroNest Support

The family water tracker for iPhone and Apple Watch

Need a hand? Email support@madebykamal.app and tell us what happened and which iPhone and iOS version you use. Replies usually arrive within two business days.

Common questions

I paid but Premium is locked

Open the Premium screen (the Premium card on the More tab) and tap Restore Purchases at the bottom, signed in with the Apple Account that made the purchase. Premium is shared through Family Sharing, so a family organizer's purchase covers the whole family.

How do I cancel or manage my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us: open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Cancelling there stops the next renewal, and Premium stays active until the end of the period you paid for.

What is free, and what is Premium?

Free forever: complete water tracking for yourself, with your goal, your streak, widgets, the Apple Watch app, reminders and your daily report.

Premium adds your whole family, pet profiles, every drink in the catalog, Mindful mode, water alarms and the full weekly and monthly reports. Prices are shown in your own currency on the purchase screen before you buy.

My reminders do not arrive

Check that notifications are allowed for HydroNest in the Settings app. Reminders only come between each person's wake and sleep hours, and they skip themselves when that person is already ahead of their day, so a quiet phone can simply mean you are on track. Everyone's reminders are listed on the Family tab under Alarms; change the hours and how often in each person's profile.

What is a water alarm?

A water alarm is a reminder that rings like an alarm in the Clock app: full screen, with sound, even when your iPhone is on Silent. Turn it on for a person on the Family tab, under Alarms, with the Ring as an alarm switch. Water alarms are part of Premium and need iOS 26 or later. The first time, HydroNest asks for permission to schedule alarms; if you declined, allow it again in the Settings app under HydroNest. Notification reminders stay free for everyone.

My widget or Apple Watch shows old numbers

Open HydroNest once on your iPhone after installing or updating so it can refresh what the widgets and the Watch show.

Apple Health

Health sync is optional: turn it on in the More tab under Sync to Apple Health. HydroNest writes the water and caffeine you log to Health and never reads anything from it. Only your own profile is written to Health, never your family members' or your pets'.

Does my data sync between my devices?

Yes, through your own private iCloud, when iCloud is on for HydroNest. Nothing passes through a server of ours; we have none. A new iPhone signed in to the same Apple Account restores your family when HydroNest first opens.

Which devices does HydroNest need?

HydroNest needs iOS 18.0 or later on iPhone or iPad, and the Apple Watch app needs watchOS 11.0 or later. Water alarms need iOS 26 or later.

A note on what HydroNest is

HydroNest is a hydration tracker, not a medical device. It does not diagnose or treat anything, and pet goals are estimates. If a condition affects how much you or your pet should drink, follow your doctor or your vet, not the app.
